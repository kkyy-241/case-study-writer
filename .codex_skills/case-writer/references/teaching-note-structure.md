# Teaching Note Structure

For this repository, use `references/format-templates/安德科铭案例使用说明.pdf` as the default teaching-note format template. Match its document style and organization unless the user requests a different template.

## Required Sections

1. 案例摘要
2. 教学目标
3. 适用课程与对象
4. 讨论题及参考答案
5. 页脚尾注或尾注区，标注来自 `background` 文件夹的资料来源

Optional when useful:

- 课前准备建议
- 课堂板书/时间安排
- 附录：计算过程、估值表、股权稀释表、现金流测算

## Case Synopsis

Summarize the case in 1-3 paragraphs:

- Company, protagonist, industry, and development stage.
- Financing need and decision pressure.
- Alternatives and central conflict.
- The open decision point where the student case ends.

Do not write a marketing abstract. Make the teaching problem explicit.

## Teaching Objectives

Use 4-7 objectives. Good objectives start with verbs:

- 理解创业企业不同成长阶段的融资需求与风险特征。
- 分析信息不对称如何影响创业企业融资渠道、估值和契约条款。
- 比较债务融资、股权融资、风险投资、可转债、优先股等工具的适用条件。
- 评估估值、股权稀释、控制权和投资人增值服务之间的权衡。
- 设计兼顾融资效率、创始人控制和投资人保护的股权与治理安排。
- 运用案例事实形成融资决策建议并识别执行风险。

## Suitable Courses And Learners

Name the likely courses:

- 创业金融
- 公司金融/公司理财
- 风险投资与私募股权
- 企业估值
- 创业管理
- 并购融资, if relevant

Name the audience:

- 商学院本科高年级学生
- 金融、管理、会计、创新创业方向硕士研究生
- MBA/EMBA, if the case complexity supports it

## Reference Answer Pattern

For each of the five questions, answer in this structure:

1. 涉及理论: Introduce the finance theory or framework in concise academic language.
2. 结合案例分析: Apply the theory to specific case facts, using numbers and terms when available.
3. 方案比较: Compare alternatives by benefits, costs, risks, incentives, and implementation conditions.
4. 课堂结论: Offer a defensible answer while acknowledging assumptions and tradeoffs.

## Required Theory Coverage

Across the five answers, cover the theories most relevant to the case:

- Entrepreneurial financing theory and startup growth stages.
- Capital structure theory and its limits in early-stage firms.
- Pecking order theory.
- Information asymmetry, adverse selection, and signaling.
- Agency theory and moral hazard.
- Venture capital screening, staged financing, monitoring, and value-added services.
- Contract design: preferred equity, convertible instruments, liquidation preference, redemption, anti-dilution, ratchets, board/veto rights.
- Valuation: pre-money/post-money, dilution, comparable valuation, DCF/scenarios, real options for high-growth ventures.
- Control rights and equity arrangements: founder control, voting rights, holding platforms, option pools, governance.
- Financing-stage matching: seed, startup, growth, expansion, pre-IPO or exit.

## Tone Difference From Student Case

The teaching note should be direct, analytical, and theory-grounded. It may reveal recommended answers and calculations. It should not continue the story style of the case narrative.

## Analytical Depth

Prioritize completeness, pedagogical depth, and analytical richness over brevity when drafting the teaching note. Do not overly constrain the length of this section. The teaching note should function as a comprehensive instructional resource for instructors who may not already possess deep expertise in the focal industry, company, or financial instruments discussed in the case.

Within this section, provide detailed explanations of all financing tools, capital structure decisions, investment mechanisms, and financial arrangements mentioned in the case narrative, including their definitions, operational mechanics, practical applications, strategic rationale, advantages, disadvantages, risks, stakeholder incentives, and implications for valuation, governance, control, liquidity, and future fundraising.

Whenever appropriate, include expanded background explanations and conceptual discussions related to venture capital, private equity, convertible notes, SAFE agreements, preferred shares, IPOs, secondary offerings, debt financing, SPVs, syndication structures, dilution, liquidation preferences, corporate governance, entrepreneurial finance, and other relevant finance concepts.

The teaching note should also proactively connect the case to broader business school learning objectives, finance theory, entrepreneurial finance frameworks, strategic decision-making, and real-world industry practices. Rich explanatory content, contextual expansion, interdisciplinary linkage, and extended conceptual discussion are strongly encouraged even if they substantially increase the overall length of this section.

## Source Notes

Use Chinese for the teaching note unless the user explicitly requests another language. For facts drawn from `background`, mark numbered superscript note markers after the cited content. If the cited content ends with a quotation mark, comma, or period, place the superscript after that punctuation; if it ends with a semicolon or colon, place the superscript before that punctuation. Preserve traceability with footer-style endnotes. If true page footers are not technically available during drafting, include a clearly labeled `资料来源尾注` section and keep each note concise.
